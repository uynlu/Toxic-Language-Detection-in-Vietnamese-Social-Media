    - Implicit
        + Implicitness means that the toxic content of the given text is subtle or indirect, so it cannot be obvious as abusive or hateful easily or immediately.
        + It uses figurative language, sarcasm, irony, wordplay, cultural references, euphemisms, indirect references, or even humor expression to convey toxic meaning.

        + Reporting refers to describing personal or second-hand experiences in a way that carry negative implications, including the act of spreading criticism, harmful narratives, or unverified rumors about others.
